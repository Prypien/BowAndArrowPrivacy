2. Worum es geht

Grow Into Life ist ein digitales Babytagebuch. Du hältst darin die Entwicklung deines Kindes fest — Meilensteine, Fotos, Maße, Zähne — und kannst das Tagebuch mit Menschen teilen, die du selbst einlädst. Die Daten liegen in deinem persönlichen Konto. Sie werden nicht verkauft, nicht an Dritte weitergegeben, nicht ausgewertet und nicht für Werbung genutzt.

Keine Werbung im Tagebuch, kein Crash-Reporting an Dritte. Die App zeigt keine Anzeigen und bindet kein Werbenetzwerk ein. Deine Einträge, Fotos und die Daten deines Kindes verlassen den Kreis der eingeladenen Personen nicht — sie werden niemals an Meta oder sonst jemanden übermittelt.

Eine Messung gibt es dennoch, und nur mit deiner Einwilligung. Wir schalten Anzeigen für die App und wollen wissen, welche davon funktioniert. Dafür fragt die App dich beim ersten Start, ob du damit einverstanden bist. Sagst du nein, wird nichts gemessen und der Meta-Baustein wird gar nicht erst gestartet. Sagst du ja, werden drei Ereignisse gemeldet — App geöffnet, Testzeit begonnen, Kauf abgeschlossen — zusammen mit der Werbekennung deines Geräts. Details stehen in Abschnitt 4a. Du kannst die Einwilligung jederzeit widerrufen: Profil → Einstellungen.

2. What this app does

Grow Into Life is a digital baby journal. You record your child’s development — milestones, photos, measurements, teeth — and you can share the journal with people you invite yourself. The data lives in your personal account. It is never sold, never shared with third parties, never profiled and never used for advertising.

No ads in the journal, no third-party crash reporting. The app displays no advertising and embeds no ad network. Your entries, photos and your child’s data never leave the circle of people you invited — they are never transmitted to Meta or anyone else.

There is measurement, and only with your consent. We run ads for the app and want to know which ones work. The app therefore asks you on first launch whether you agree. If you decline, nothing is measured and the Meta component is never started. If you agree, three events are reported — app opened, trial started, purchase completed — together with your device’s advertising identifier. Details in section 4a. You can withdraw consent at any time under Profile → Settings.

4. Where the data is stored

The app uses Google Firebase as a processor (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Cloud Firestore for journal data (stored in europe-west3, Frankfurt, Germany) and Firebase Storage for photos. A data processing agreement is in place; EU standard contractual clauses cover any third-country transfers. Payments for the full version are handled entirely by Apple — we never receive payment details, only whether a valid purchase exists.

4a. Ad measurement with Meta — only after consent

We run ads for Grow Into Life. To find out which ad actually led to an install, the app may use the Facebook SDK from Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, 4 Grand Canal Square, Dublin 2, Ireland.

  • What is sent: three events — app opened (Session), trial started (StartTrial) and purchase completed (Purchase, with amount and currency) — plus your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address and device type.
  • What is not sent: nothing from the journal. No names, no dates of birth, no photos, no milestones, no measurements, no tooth data, no comments. Meta does not learn that you have a child, let alone what it is called.
  • Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and § 25(1) TDDDG for access to the advertising identifier.
  • When: only after you agreed in the dialog on first launch. Before that the SDK is not even initialised — not a single byte reaches Meta.
  • Withdrawal: at any time under Profile → Settings, via a switch, exactly as easy as giving consent. You can also turn tracking off system-wide under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Third-country transfer: Meta also processes data in the USA, based on the EU standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. A residual risk of access by US authorities cannot be fully excluded; by consenting you accept that risk.
  • Joint controllership: for this measurement we and Meta are joint controllers (Art. 26 GDPR). Meta’s privacy policy: facebook.com/privacy/policy

The app itself displays no advertising. This is purely about measuring whether our own ads are worth their money.

5. Who can see the data

Only you and the people you add via an invitation code. Access is enforced server-side: without a membership in a baby book, its data cannot be read. You can revoke access at any time in the app; the person then loses access and the local copy on their device is removed on next launch.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your user profile, your baby books including milestones, photos and measurements, your memberships and your sign-in account. The operation cannot be undone.

7. Device permissions

Photo library — only to attach pictures you select to a milestone. Camera — only to take a photo directly in the app. Both are used solely in response to your explicit action; the app does not read the photo library in the background.

8. This website

These pages are served via GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., San Francisco, USA), which processes technically necessary server log data including your IP address (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Fonts are self-hosted, no cookies are set and no analytics are used.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). You may withdraw consent — in particular for health data — at any time with future effect by deleting the entries in question. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
